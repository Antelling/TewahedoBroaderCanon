# Section I (continued): Love the Lord; the Rich and the Holy Scriptures

**Continues** `02-children-of-light-and-purity.md` (which ended at the rules on hair and
apparel, [i., 3]). This file covers Harden's **[i., 4]** (created in God's image; the drunkard;
the word of Christ; Deut 6:5–7) and **CHAPTER II / [i., 5]** ("That the rich ought to seek to
know the profit of the Scriptures at all times"), closing with "being then acquainted with
these, thou shalt be strong in the right faith."
**Sigla:** P = Platt 1834 · H = Harden 1920 · AC = Apostolic Constitutions.
Ge'ez restorations from OCR noise are marked ⌈thus⌉; unrecoverable words as […?]; conjectural
restorations are marked **(conj.)**. Harden's section-numbering [i., 4]–[i., 5] and his
CHAPTER II heading are reproduced; P's corresponding heading is his "I. That it is the duty of
the Rich to search into the profit of the Scriptures at all times, (in the strength of) the
Holy Spirit."

## 14. [i., 4.] Created in His image; not a drunkard; the word of Christ

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored):**

> ⋯ ⊦⌈ግዚአ⌉ብሔር ⌈ዘፈጠረከ⌉ ⋯ ⌈በጽቡሖ⌉ ወ⌈በኣምሳሊሁ⌉። ወእመ ⌈ትፈትው⌉ ከመ ⌈ትሕመድ⌉ ⊦⌈ለ⌉ከ⌈አ⌉ብሔር ⋯ ⌈አትገብ⌉ር ⌈ዘእድያ⌉
> ወኢትብ⌈ዕ⌉ ሰ⌈ካራ⌉ ⋯ ወኢ⌈ትሖለፍ⌉ ውስተ ⌈ረሓብ⌉ ምስለ ⌈ዘሙታን⌉ እ⌈ንበለ⌉ ⌥⌈በብ⌉ ወ⌈ኣእምር⌉። ⌈ውእቱ⌉ ⌈ተፈትው⌉ ⌈ከመ⌉
> ⌈ትሕመድ⌉ ⊦⌈ግዚአ⌉ብ⌈ሔ⌉∭ ⋯ ⌈ትንበር⌉ በ⌈ገብረ⌉ እ⌈ደ⌉ከ ⋯ ወ⌈ወተር⌉ ቃ⌈ሎ⌉ ⊠⌈የ⌉ሱ∵ ⌈ክር∵⌉⌈ቶ⌉∵ ⌈ቡ⌉ሎ ⌈ጊዜ⌉። እ⌈ስመ⌉
> ⌈ጽሑፍ⌉ ⌈ደብ⌉ል ⌈መጽ⌉∐⍈ ሕ⌈ጉ⌉ ስ⌈መ⌉ ⊦⌈ግዚአ⌉≥⌈ሔ⌉∭ አ⌈ም⌉∋⌈ክ⌉ከ ⌈ማ⋕∍ተ⌉ ወ⌈ሌሊ⌉ተ ⋯ ≠⌈ፍ⊖ተ⌉ ⌈እለ⌉ ≠⌈ው∵≰⌉ ⌈ቤት⌉
> ⋯ ከመ ⌈ትርከብ⌉ ⋯ ወ⌈ምሕሪ⌉ተ።

**Translation:**

> … God, who created thee ⌈after His image⌉ and ⌈after His likeness⌉. And if thou desirest to
> please the Lord God, do not ⌈evil⌉; and be not a drunkard, nor walk in the streets with the
> adulterers, without wisdom and understanding. But be thou ⌈pleasing⌉ to God the Lord …, and
> ⌈live⌉ by the work of thine hands …, and remember the word of Jesus Christ at all times. For
> the Scripture speaketh of him who meditates in the book of the law of God his Maker, day and
> night, ⌈whether in the way⌉ or in the house …, that thou mayest walk … and ⌈find⌉ mercy.

**Notes:**
1. P: "And if thou doest thus, thou art become cast out from the Law, and far from God, who
   created thee in His image and in His likeness. But, if thou desirest to please the Lord, do
   not that which is bad; and put far from thee evil, and all that thy God hateth. And be not a
   drunkard, and walk not in the streets with the adulterers, without wisdom and understanding;
   but be thou obedient, and live by the work of thine hands, and seek to do every thing that is
   pleasing to God, and remember the word of our Lord Jesus Christ, and follow him at all
   times." H (p. 6): "art a transgressor of the law, and God created thee in His image … how
   desirest thou to please God, do all that thy God hateth. [i., 4.] Be not a drunkard, and walk
   not in the streets … without wisdom and knowledge … pleasing to God; and remember Jesus, and
   follow Him at all times."
2. "the word of Jesus Christ at all times": Ge'ez ⌈ወወተር ቃሎ ኢየሱስ ክርስቶስ ቡሎ ጊዜ⌉ (conj.);
   P "remember the word of our Lord Jesus Christ, and follow him at all times"; H "remember
   Jesus, and follow Him at all times."
3. The meditation clause = Dt 6:6–7 / Ps 1:2 ("day and night"); Harden references Ps 1, 2;
   Dt 6, 5 sq. P: "whether on the way or in the house, and even when thou sleepest, love the
   Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy mind, and with all thy strength, that thou
   mayest find from Him mercy and compassion"; H: "whether in the way, or in the house, or thou
   sleepest, love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy mind and with all thy
   strength, that thou mayest find from Him compassion and mercy." The explicit Dt 6:5 citation
   is not recovered from the noisy Ge'ez print […?]; the restored clause "day and night … in the
   way … in the house" is supported by ገማዕልተ ወሌሊተ ("day and night") and በፍኖተ ("in the way").
   Not in AC.

## 15. CHAPTER II — [i., 5.] That the rich ought to seek the profit of the Scriptures at all times

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored; the print here repeats one run, see note 6):**

> ⌈እመ⌉ ⌈ዝንቱ⌉ ⌈እንተ⌉ ⌈ዓሪብ⌉ ⋯ ወአ⌈ትፈ⌉≅⌈ድ⌉ ⌈ኪነ⌉ ወ⌈ቅኔ⌉ እ⌈ደ⌉ከ ⋯ ወኢ⌈ትንበ⌉ር ⌈ባዕል⌉ አ⌈ቲ⌉ ሎ⌈ቱ⌉ ን⌈ጹ∓⌉ን። ቦ⌈ቱ⌉
> ⌈ኣቲ⌉ ወ⌈ይንበ⌉ር ም∵⌈ለሆ⌉∙ ⌈መ⌉∵⌈ለ⌉ ⌈እ⌈ሎ⌉ የ⌈ገብ⌉∩ ⌈ም∵⌈ለ⌉ከ ዘ⌈ደማእከ⌉ለ በ⌈እ⌈ም⊐⌉ቲሆ∙። ወ⌈የናገር⌉ ⌈ነገሪ⌉ ሕ⌈ደ⌉ወት
> ወ⌈አ⊕ብ≥⌉ ዘ⌈ው∵⌉≰ ሕ⌈ገ⌉ ⌈መጻሕፍት⌉ ⌈ነገሥት⌉ ወ⌈ነቢያ⌉ት ⋯ ጽ⌈ሑፈ⌉ ወ⌈ስብ⌉∐≰ ⋯ ዘ⌈ውኳ⌉≱ ⌈ጽድ≅⌉ ባ⌈ተ⌉ ≠⌈ሉ⌉
> ⋯ ወረ⌈ሕቅ⌉ ⌈ኣ⌉∝≡⌈ሉ⌉ ⌅⌈ኔ⌉ ⌈አ∛∍⊭≰⌉ ⌈አ∕⋛≣⌉≥። ወ⌈ኢ⋨⌈ጽህ⌉≀ ⌈ከን≶⌉ ⌈አል≦⌉ ወ⌈ኢ≵∙⌈ሌ⌉ሥ⌈ሥ⌉ ⌈ካል⌉⊥ ጽሑፈ።
> ⌈እ⌈ስመ⌉ ⌈ለ⊥⌈ሎ⌉ ⌈ኢ≵∭⌈ከብ⌉ ው∵≰ ⌈ሕ⌈ገ⌉ ⊦⌈ግዚአ⌉≥⌈ሔ⌉∭ ⊨⌈መ⌉ ∝⌈ን∝⌉ ⌈ት∝∅⌈ር⌉≰ ⌈አ∕⋛≣⌉≥። ወ⌈ሓ∘⌉ ⌈ት⍈≅ድ⌉
> ⌈ተ⌉⊠⌈ም⌉∭ ዘ⌈ደ⌉∈ ⌈ጥ⌉∭⌈ጊ⌉ ⌈መ⌈ጻ⌉∕⍈≵ ∥∥ ⌈መጽ∐ፌ⌉ ⌈ገ∠≵⌉ ወ⊥⌈መዛ⌉ ⌈ጽህ⌉≀ ም⌈ስ⌉∈ ⌈ጥ≠≥⌉ ⌈ጠ≢≣⊕⌉ ⋈⌈ት∝∅⌈ር⌉≶∙
> ≥⌈ከ⌉ ⌈ነ≢⋨≰⌉ ⌈ኢ⋮≥⌉ ወ⌈ካ∎⌉∎⌈ሞ⊕⌉። ⌈ይ⌉∉ ≥⌈እ∲⌉ ⌈ጥ≠≥⌉ ⋈⌈ው∵≰⌉ ⌈ተ⌉∙⌈ር⌉ በ⌈ኘ≤⌉ ⌈ው∝⌉ር። ⌈እ⌈ሉ⌉ ⌈የ⍈⋩⍈⋰⌉
> ∈≥⋈⌈ሀ⌉ ⋈⌈ም⊕⌉∝ ⌈መ⌉∝∅⌈ራ⌉⊕ ⋘⌈ረ⊢⌉⋨⌈ሙ⌉ ባ⌈ሕ≲≱⌉ ⌈ጠ≢≠⌉ ⌈ጠ≢≣⊕⌉ ።

**Translation:**

> If thou in this world art rich ⌈and hast no need⌉ of ⌈the craft⌉ and ⌈toil⌉ of thine hands …,
> and be not slothful, sitting idle. Go thou to the ⌈faithful⌉, and sit thou with them, with
> those who ⌈are thy fellows⌉, that are united with thee in their faith. And speak ⌈the word of
> life⌉, and read what is in the law, of Kings ⌈and Prophets⌉ …, and ⌈sing a psalm⌉ of ⌈praise⌉
> …; and be thou far from all ⌈service⌉ of the gods of the Gentiles. And be not engaged in ⌈vain
> words⌉ …, neither seek ⌈another⌉ scripture. For what is there that thou findest not in the law
> of God the Lord, that thou seekest ⌈the teaching of the Gentiles⌉? And again, if thou desirest
> to know the power ⌈of the interpretation⌉ of the Scriptures, seek out ⌈the Book of Kings⌉; and
> if thou wishest ⌈the wisdom⌉ of the wise and their teaching, thou hast the prophets, Job and
> Solomon; ⌈these⌉ say: Blessed is the man ⌈whose delight is in the law of the Lord⌉ …, who is
> ⌈full of wisdom⌉ … . ⌈These⌉ are ⌈more⌉ than ⌈all teachers⌉, that with them thou mayest find
> knowledge, ⌈more than all the wise⌉.

**Notes:**
1. H, CHAPTER II: "THAT THE RICH OUGHT TO SEEK TO KNOW THE PROFIT [OF THE SCRIPTURES] AT ALL
   TIMES IN THE STRENGTH [OF THE HOLY SPIRIT]"; [i., 5.]: "But if thou art rich, and hast no
   need of the art and labour of thy hand, wherewith to earn thy bread and livelihood, be not
   slothful, nor sit idle, but go to the faithful, and sit with them … speak with another …
   Kings and the Prophets … psalm of praise … the word of the Gospel, which is the
   [completion]…". P: "If then thou art rich, and needest not the art and labour of thy hands,
   with which to get thy bread and to live, be not slothful and sit not idle; but go to the
   faithful, and sit with them, with those that are united with thee in the faith; and talk of
   the word of life, and read that which is in the Scriptures, the Law, the Kings, and the
   Prophets; and sing a Psalm of praise, and listen to the word of the Gospel, which is the
   completion of the whole."
2. "the word of the Gospel, which is the completion of the whole" (P): the corresponding Ge'ez
   is not recovered from the print […?]; H likewise reads "which is the completion of the
   whole" (his text: "marked to the word of the Gospel, which is the [com]pletion of the
   whole"). The clause stands in the Syriac; it should be present in the Ge'ez.
3. "For what is there that thou findest not in the law of God…": Ge'ez ⌈እስመ ለእለ ኢትርከብ ውስተ
   ሕገ እግዚአብሔር ከመ ምንም ትምህርተ ኣሕዛብ⌉; H: "What then art thou unable (to find) in the law of
   God, that thou shouldest seek after the learning of the Gentiles?"
4. "seek out the Book of Kings": Ge'ez ⌈መጽሐፌ ገሠት⌉ restored (⌈መጽሐፌ ነገሥት⌉, "book of Kings");
   P mistranslates "the Book of Kings" as "the Books of Moses" later in the passage; H notes
   "P mistranslates."
5. "thou hast the prophets, Job and Solomon": H "the prophets, Job and Solomon. These are they
   that are full of wisdom, and thou shalt find knowledge in them. These, more than all
   teachers, shall make understanding to increase, for their word is the voice of the Lord God,
   who alone is all-wise." P: "these are they who are filled with wisdom, and with them thou
   shalt find knowledge. These shall increase understanding more than all teachers; for their
   words are the voice of the Lord God, who alone is Most Wise." Ge'ez ⌈እሉ የፈዩፍደ ለብወ ምንም
   መምህራን ዘረኢየሙ ባሕቲቱ ጠቢበ ጠቢባን⌉ = "these, more than all teachers, … with them, more than all
   the wise." The "voice of the Lord God" clause (Ge'ez ⌈ውኣዕቱ ⋯ ለእግዚአብሔር⌉, at the head of the
   next print-block) belongs here.
6. **Print disorder.** The Platt print here repeats one run — "read what is in Deuteronomy …
   the glory of God, who saved thee from many snares of Satan … the Saviour came not to destroy
   the law and the prophets … Come unto me … read the Law and the Prophets and the Books of the
   Kings and the Gospel … righteous men who reigned … evil kings … have lost eternal life"
   — appearing both before and after the "Book of Kings / Job and Solomon" run (two adjacent
   print-blocks of the 1834 edition overlap). The run is translated once here for the sequence
   in which P's English and H agree: "Most Wise. And if thou desirest a song, thou hast the
   Psalms of David. If again thou desirest to understand the creation of the world and the
   generation of the ancient fathers, thou hast the Books of Moses [H: thou hast the Law]. And
   if thou desirest to know wisdom and doctrine, thou hast the book of the law, which is full of
   the glory of the Lord God. Keep far, then, from works of evil, and from the doctrine of
   Satan, and the word of vanity, which is not in the law; but read what is in Deuteronomy [P:
   the Law of Ceremonies], that thou mayest understand and know the glory of God, who saved thee
   from many snares of Satan. [For the Saviour came not to destroy the Law and the Prophets,
   with H's lacuna noted,] but to fulfil them, and to loose the bonds which are in Deuteronomy
   [P: the Law of Ceremonies], and to bring men to a spiritual teaching. Wherefore He calleth
   us, saying, Come unto me all ye that are troubled and heavy laden, and I will give you rest
   (Mt 11:28). And thou, when thou hast read the Law and the Prophets and the Books of the Kings
   and the Gospel, shalt understand how many were the righteous men who have reigned and pleased
   God in their lives, and for this have found life eternal; and also thou shalt know how many
   evil kings have transgressed through lack of understanding. These have perished and been
   destroyed by the stroke of the judgment of God, and have lost eternal life." Ge'ez anchors
   for this run: ⌈እላ አንብብ ዘውስተ ደገም ሕግ ⋯ ወትጠደቅ ስብሐተ ዚአ እግዚአብሔር ዘአድኖሣነከ አመ ብዙእ መሣገራሆ ለሰይጣን⌉;
   ⌈እስመ እሎቱ መሐላዊ ድፍንሠቶሙ ለነገሥት ወለነቢያት ከመ ይፈጽሞሙ ወደፍታ ለነ መአኣሲ ዘውስተ ሥርዓተ ሕግ ወየገብከብዶሙ ውስተ
   ትምህርተ መንፈስ ቅዱስ⌉ ("to bring men to a spiritual teaching"); ⌈እሎቱ ደጺውዐን ⋯ ደብል ሎቱ ገቤሮ ልካ ጽዑርን
   ኣምል ጾር ወአነ አዕርፈክሙው⌉ (Mt 11:28, "come unto me… I will give you rest"); ⌈ወአንተ እምከመ አንብብ
   ካልእ ወነቢየተ ወነገሥተ ወወንጌለ ⋯ ዕደው ጻድቀን ውስተ ጽድቅ ወየሠሐመርወ ለግዚአብሔር ወለዝንቱ ረከቡ ሕይወተ ዘለዓለም⌉;
   ⌈ወባሕቱ ተአመር ስፍነ ነገሠት ርኩባን እለ ዐለው ታሕተ እግዚአብሔር በኢለብወቶሙ⌉. The Ge'ez for "Psalms of
   David" and "books of Moses" in this run is not recovered from the print […?].

## 16. Close: strong in the right faith [i., 5 end]

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored):**

> ⌈እመ⌉ አ⌈ምር⌉ክ∙ ዜ⌈ንቲ⌉አ ⌈ትኩን⌉ ር⌈ቱዕ⌉ ው∵ተ ⌈ርትዕ⌉ ⌈እምነት⌉ ወ⌈ትርከብ⌉ ⌈ሞገሰ⌉ ≠⌈ኘ≠⌉ ⊦⌈ግዚአ⌉≥⌈ሔ⌉∭ ⌈ክ⌉∭∵⌈ቶ⌉∵።
> ⌈እ⌈ስ⌉∘⌉ ⌈ንሕ⊐⌉ ⌈ሎ≱⌉ ⌈አ≣∎⌉።

**Translation:**

> Being then acquainted with these, thou shalt be strong in the right faith, and shalt find
> grace with ⌈our Lord⌉ Jesus Christ; for thou art one of his members.

**Notes:**
1. P: "Being then acquainted with these, thou shalt be strong in the right faith, and shalt
   find grace and favour with Jesus Christ; for thou art become one of his members"; H (p. 8):
   "strong in the right faith, and shalt find grace and favour with our Lord Jesus Christ, for
   thou art one of His members."
2. "grace": Ge'ez ⌈ሞገሰ⌉ (grace/mercy) restored; the formula closes both P's Chapter II and H's
   [i., 5], and leads directly into the bath-warning and Proverbs vii (next file,
   `04-the-bath-and-the-strange-woman.md`).
