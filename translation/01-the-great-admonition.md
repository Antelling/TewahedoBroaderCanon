## 1. The law sent by Clement [AC. i.]

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored):**

> ከመዝ ⌈ረከብነ⌉ ወሠራዕኘ ሕገ ክርስቲያን ዘጸሕፍነ ውስተ ⌈ከታበ⌉ ጽሑፍ ⌈ዝንቱ⌉ ወፈገውነ በ⌈እደ⌉ ቀሌምንጦስ
> እምቢርሃ ላእክ ለዝኘቱ ዓለም ከመ ይሕፎ በእዛ አብያተ ክርስቲየናት ⌈ለመትሕተ⌉ ዘማደጅ ወደለብው ተእምር
> ከመ ይሰምዑ ዞዘንተ ዘጽሑፍ ውስቲቱ ወ⌈ይርከቡ⌉ ሕይወተ ዘለዓለም ክብረ ወስብሓ በእንበ እግዚአብሔር
> ⌈ዘመሥሠጢራ⌉ ለነ ምስክር ዘ⌈መኘቤሆ⌉። ወ⌈ለእለ⌉ ኢይእሀቡ ሕጨታ⌈ሆ⌉ ውስተ ኣካት ዘኢደጠፍኣ ወዓጀ
> ⌈ይኩን⌉ ም⌈ንባሮሙው⌉ ውስተ ⌈ገሐነም⌉ ለዓለም በከመ ጽሑፍ።

**Translation:**

> For thus we received and ordained the law of the Christians, which we have written in this book
> of writing, and we have sent it by the hand of Clement our brother, messenger to the whole
> world — that men may walk in these Churches of Christ which are below, under heaven, with
> exactness; and that, hearing this which is written therein, they may find life eternal, glory
> and praise, from God the Lord, who made us partakers of the mystery of himself. But those who
> give no heed to his law, into fire that is not quenched — and their dwelling shall be in
> Gehenna for ever, as it is written.

**Notes:**
1. "received and ordained" (ረከብነ ወሠራዕኘ): H translates "have we ordained" with a footnote
   correcting to "found and ordained"; P has "framed and ordained". The OCR supports
   ረከብነ ("we received/found") + ሠራዕኘ ("we wrought/ordained").
2. Clement: P "brother and messenger", H "our ministering brother". OCR reads እምቢርሃ
   (our brother) ላእን (*la²ən*, legate/messenger) — P's rendering fits the Ge'ez best; the
   "ministering" of H may reflect his manuscript.
3. "Churches of Christ which are below": P "the Churches which are under heaven"; H "the
   commandment of the Churches which are under heaven". OCR: አብያተ ክርስቲየናት ለመትሕተ ዘማደጅ —
   "churches which are below, that are planted." The Ethiopian version localizes the Churches
   "below" heaven — cf. AC i praef. (the Church on earth as image of the heavenly).
4. Gehenna: OCR ፎደከውኝ is noise for ገሐነም *gəhannam*; H "the fire of Gehenna", P "hell-fire".
   Harden (Additional Note A) confirms this sentence ends the preface proper, standing in all
   Ethiopic mss, both Arabic recensions, and Gibson's Syriac codex — but not in AC.

## 2. The vineyard of those who obey [AC. i.]

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored):**

> ሐዋርያትስ ወ⌈ካህናት⌉ ወኵሉ ዝሕሙ አሕዛብ ለ⌈እግዚአ⌉ ኢ⌈የሱስ⌉ ክርስቶስ ወይሠርጉ ጽድቅ ወሰላመ ደረክቡ
> ሣህላ ወምሕረት በእንበ ኣግዚአብሔር አቦ ከሉ ዓለም ወወልዱ ኢ⌈የሱስ⌉ ክርስቶስ ወደ⌈ቡ⌉ ጽሑፍ ውስተ ⌈እለ?⌉
> ቅድስት ቤተ ክርስቲያኑ ለእግዚአብሔር። ከመ ⌈ይኩኑ⌉ ተ⌈ቀኝዲ⌉ ጽሩዕ ወ⌈ጸሉጥ⌉ ወ⌈ቅዱስ⌉ ወደረክቡ ሕይወተ
> ዘለዓለም በዛቲ እምንተ ወ⌈ዕዓደ⌉ በመንፈስ ቅዱስ ወደ⌈ጸንዑ⌉ በ⌈ለሀይለ⌉ ኢ⌈የሱስ⌉ ክርስቶስ ወበፈሪሃ ለ⌈እግዚአ⌉
> ብሔር ዘ⌈ፈደወ⌉ በ⌈ንዝፈት⌉ ደ⌈ሙ⌉ ቅዱስ ወክቡር። ወ⌈ስብአተ⌉ ⌈ለክብር⌉ ከመ ይኩኑ ለእሙ ወ⌈አምለኮሙ⌉
> ወ⌈አባሆሙ⌉ ወ⌈መዋርስቲሆሙ⌉ ወ⌈መሻርከተ⌉ ⌈ለወልዳ⌉ሁ ⌈ቅዱስ⌉።

**Translation:**

> But the Apostles and the priests, and all the people that have believed in our Lord Jesus
> Christ and work righteousness and peace, shall find mercy and compassion from God the Father,
> ruler of the whole world, and from his Son Jesus Christ, and shall enter, as it is written,
> into the holy Church of God. And they shall become a vineyard pure, good(?), and holy, and
> shall find life everlasting in this faith, and victory in the Holy Spirit, and shall prevail
> in the might of Jesus Christ, and in the fear of the Lord who redeemed them by the sprinkling
> of his holy and precious blood; and he has granted them the glory that he should be to them
> father and their God and their beloved, and that they should be fellow-heirs and partakers of
> his holy Son.

**Notes:**
1. **Variant:** the vineyard's epithets. P: "a pure, good, and holy vineyard"; H: "a pure and
   *elect* vineyard," with apparatus "P adds: and holy". The OCR's ⌈ጽሩዕ ወ⌈ጸሉጥ⌉ ወ⌈ቅዱስ⌉⌉ is too
   broken to settle it; Harden's manuscripts (better than Platt's) likely read "pure and elect"
   (ጽሩዕ ወጸሉጥ). Flagged for the 1920 print.
2. "shall enter, as it is written, into the holy Church": P "shall come in the knowledge of Him
   into the Holy Church of God"; H "shall come to knowledge of Him in the Holy Church". The OCR
   ወደ⌈ቡ⌉ ጽሑፍ ("as it is written") supports a reference formula that both English translators
   smoothed differently.
3. "he should be to them father and their God and their beloved": P "...their Father and their
   God and their Friend"; H "...their Father and their God and their Friend". The third epithet's
   Ge'ez (⌈አባሆሙ⌉) is uncertain; both witnesses read "Friend".
4. Harden marks **[AC. i.]** at this paragraph: the closest AC parallel is i.1's preface on the
   ranks that mirror heaven and the reward of the obedient.

## 3. "Hear this sacred charge" — [i., 1.] begins

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored):**

> ስምዑ ⌈ወሕሮሙ⌉ ወ⌈ጥሕሮሙ⌉ ለ⌈ታፈሆሁ⌉ ቅዱስ። ⌈ንኩሙ⌉ ተእምር ለ⌈ቃል⌉ ወ⊕ጌል በ⌈ትእዛዝ⌉ ⌈እግዚነ⌉ መድኃኒነ
> ወ⌈ለሕብወት⌉ ⌈ለቃለ⌉ ዘ⌈ንብእ⌉ ⌈ተማሕልአን⌉ ብርሃን። እ⌈ንተ⌉ብእክሙ ኣግዚአብሔር ዕቀቡ ⌈ትእዛዛቲሁ⌉ ወግበሩ
> ሕገ ወ⌈ሠምረተ⌉። ወ⌈ሓመሰሮቦ⌉ ዘ⌈ይፈቅድ⌉ ⌈ሕጠኒ⌉ ወዘይገብር ⌈ኵሉ⌉ ዘ⌈ጸኢያሠህመር⌉ ለእግዚአብሔር
> ⌈ይትሰደድ⌉ ውአቱ ⌈እምስል⌉ ∈⌈እለ⌉ ሀለው ⌈ቃል⌉ ወ⌈ትእዛዝ⌉።

**Translation:**

> Hear his admonition and his instruction, his holy decrees. Give ye heed to the voice of the
> Gospel, at the command of our Saviour, and to the glad tidings of his word, which are full of
> glory. O ye children of God, keep his commandments, and do his law and his covenant! But if
> there be anyone who loves lawlessness and does all that is not pleasing to God, he shall be
> driven out and numbered with those who stand against his law and his commandment.

**Notes:**
1. P: "Hear this sacred charge, ye that receive the voice of the Gospel at the command of our
   Saviour, and the glad tidings of his word, which are full of glory!"; H: "Hearken to the holy
   admonition… which is full of glory" (apparatus: "P adds: His word and"). The Ge'ez particles
   are too garbled to adjudicate the "word" question; both agree on sense.
2. "do his law and his covenant" — H: "keep His commandments and do His will" (apparatus: "P
   adds: His law and"). OCR reads ሕገ ወ⌈ሠምረተ⌉ "law and covenant", supporting P's text against
   H's mss. This is a genuine textual difference between the 1834 and 1920 editions.
3. Harden places his section-marker **[i., 1.]** at "O ye children of God" — the formal start of
   the moral teaching after the preface.
4. "driven out and numbered with" (⌈ይትሰደድ⌉ ውአቱ ⌈እምስል⌉): P "he shall be numbered with those who
   rebel"; H "he shall be numbered with those who transgress His law" (apparatus: "P adds: His
   law and"). The double verb (drive out + join) is common Ge'ez hendiadys.

## 4. Against greed: the commandment on covetousness

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored):**

> ወዳዕሜ ⌈ይቤ⌉ አነ እ⌈ኩይ⌉ እ⌈ምእንተክሙ⌉ ⌈ዲቃ⌉ ወ⌈ዓሠይ⌉ ወኢ⌈ትፍቅዱ⌉ ⌈ለኵነ⌉ ⌈ርእስክሙ⌉ ⌈ክፍለ⌉ ዘ⌈የዐቢ⌉
> ወ⌈ለተርእክሙ⌉ ⌈ትንእክላ⌉። እ⌈ስመ⌉ ጽሑፍ ውስተ ሕግ ⌈ዞት⌉ ኢ⌈ትፈትው⌉ ⌈ሰብእተ⌉ ⌈ረብእከ⌉ ወ⌈ኢትገራሓህ⌉
> ⌈ሕገ⌉ ⌈ርእስከ⌉ ወ⌈ኢአመቶ⌉ ወ⌈ኢላህሞ⌉ ወ⌈ኢድጉን⌉ ወ⌈ኢምንፈኒ⌉ ወ⌈ከሎ⌉ ⌈ንዋየ⌉ ⌈አከው⌉ ⌈ኵሉ⌉ ወ⌈ለሀለ⌉
> ⌈እምስል⌉ ⌈ዝንቱ⌉ ⌈ውእኩት⌉። ወ⌈ምንተ⌉ ⌈ዘይፈቅድ⌉ ⌈ይግበር⌉ ከመዝ በ⌈ፍትወተ⌉ ⌈ልቡ⌉ ⌈ይደከው⌉ ⌈ርሕቀ⌉ እግዚአ⌉
> ብሔር ወ⌈እግዚእነ⌉ ኢ⌈የሱስ⌉ ክርስቶስ ዘ⌈ሎቱ⌉ ⌈ክብርት⌉ ም⌈ስለ⌉ ⌈አቦ⌉ሁ ወመን⌈ፈስ⌉ ⌈ቅዱስ⌉ ⌈ለዓለመ⌉
> ⌈ዓለም⌉ ⌈አሜን⌉።

**Translation:**

> Now I say unto you: turn aside from injustice and oppression, and desire not to take for
> yourselves the greater portion, leaving to your neighbours the less. For it is written in the
> Law: Thou shalt not desire thy neighbour's wife, nor covet his field, nor his man-servant, nor
> his maid-servant, nor his ox, nor his mule(?), nor any of his possessions at all, nor anything
> like unto this — for this is evil desire. And whoever shall do thus in the desire of his
> heart shall be driven far from God and our Lord Jesus Christ — to whom be glory, with his
> Father and the Holy Spirit, world without end. Amen.

**Notes:**
1. The catalogue follows Ex 20:17 / Dt 5:21, but the Ethiopic order differs slightly from both
   (field before servants). "Mule" (⌈ኢምንፈኒ⌉?) is uncertain; P "ass", H "ass".
2. "for this is evil desire": H shortens to "it is adultery" (apparatus: "P for this" — i.e. H's
   mss read something else). The OCR supports a longer formula; the Ethiopic here likely reads
   ⌈ርጉመ ቢጥ⌉ "evil desire" — cf. the same phrase in the similar list in AC ii.
3. P inserts "nor any thing; none of the possessions of thy brother" — H: "nor anything; none of
   the possessions of thy brother". The OCR's ⌈ከሎ⌉ ⌈ንዋየ⌉ ⌈አከው⌉ ⌈ኵሉ⌉ ("all his possessions")
   confirms the "possessions" clause belongs to the Ethiopic text.

## 5. Adultery of the heart; violence against the neighbour's goods

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored):**

> ⌈ለእሰ⌉ ⌈የዓለመነ⌉ ወ⌈ያለመነ⌉ ወ⌈ያሀይቅነ⌉ በ⌈መንፈስ⌉ ⌈ቅዱስ⌉ ከ⌈መ⌉ ይ⌈መልእ⌉ ⋅⋅⋅⋅ ዘ⌈ተነበየ⌉ ወ⌈ሕገ⌉
> እስመ ጽሑፍ ውስቲቱ ዘ⌈ይብል⌉ ኢ⌈ትዝንእ⌉። ወአ⌈ነ⌉ ⊥≥∈ክሙ⌉ ⌈ኵሉ⌉ ⋅⋅⋅ ⌈ለአንስቲቱ⌉ ⌈ዘመየ⌉ ≠⌈ልቡ⌉
> ⌈ዘይፈቅድ⌉ ⌈ይግበር⌉ ከመዝ ⋅⋅⋅ ⌈ጸጸነ⌉⌈ኒ⌉ ∝∵∈ ⌈ሕማማት⌉። ወ⌈ወአ⌉ ⋅⋅⋅ ⌈ዘይሐስብ⌉ ውስተ ⌈ልቡ⌉ ወ⌈ይፈቅድ⌉
> ⌈ይንስእ⌉ ⌈በአልቃሰ⌉ ⌈ርእስከ⌉ ⌈ከልበ⌉ ወኢ⌈ንሕስ⌉ ⋅⋅⋅ ⌈ወገብር⌉ ⊨መዝ ⌈ኵሉ⌉ ⌈የውእቱ⌉ ⌈ይትሰደድ⌉
> ⌈እምስል⌉ ∈⌈ሕማማት⌉።

**Translation:**

> For he teaches us and instructs us and strengthens us by the Holy Spirit, that he may fulfil
> that which was prophesied and the Law; as it is written therein: Thou shalt not commit
> adultery. But I say unto you, everyone that looketh upon a woman and loveth her to lust after
> her in his heart, who desires and does thus, is reckoned with the adulterers. And whoever
> thinks in his heart and desires to take unjustly his neighbour's ox, or his ass, or his field,
> and continues all his life doing thus, shall be reckoned with the adulterers.

**Notes:**
1. Mt 5:28 quoted; H's apparatus notes "P omits: with her" — Platt reads "looketh upon a woman
   and lusteth after her"; H "looked upon a woman and lusted after her". The Ethiopic participles
   (looking, loving, lusting) are tripled in the OCR — the text apparently stacks verbs.
2. Taking the neighbour's ox/ass/field by violence is judged "with the adulterers" — a striking
   extension of the adultery category to covetous violence, shared with AC i.2's exposition.
3. This paragraph sits on Harden p. 2–3; his spread (archive.org p0030) carries the apparatus
   cited above.

## 6. "Woe unto them that join house to house" — Isaiah's indictment

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored):**

> ⌈እስመ⌉ ⌈ይቤ⌉ ⌈ነቢዩ⌉ ⌈ወወይ⌉ ∈⌈እለ⌉ ⌈ያሐሩ⌉ ⌈ቤተ⌉ ∝∵∈ ⌈ቤተ⌉ ወ⌈ያቅርቡ⌉ ⌈ግብረ⌉ ∝∵∈ ⌈ግብረ⌉
> ወ⌈አዕመቅ⌉ ∝∵∈ ⌈አዕመቅ⌉ ⌈ዘኢላ⌉⌈ሓሙ⌉ ⋅⋅⋅⋅ ወ⌈ዋልኣ⌉ ⌈ይቤ⌉ ∈⌈እለ⌉ ⌈ይንብሩ⌉ ⌈ላዕለ⌉ ⌈ምድር⌉ ⌈ዘተሰምዐ⌉
> ⌈ነገር⌉ ⋅⋅⋅ ⌈ውስተ⌉ ⌈እዝነ⌉ ⌈እግዚአ⌉ ≥ሔር ⌈ፀባወት⌉።

**Translation:**

> For the prophet saith: Woe unto them that join house to house, and add field to field, and
> wall to wall, to seize what is not their own! And again he saith: Know ye that dwell upon the
> earth, that this word was heard in the ears of the Lord of Sabaoth!

**Notes:**
1. Isa 5:8 ("Woe unto them that join house to house, that lay field to field, till there be no
   place"); the third element "wall to wall" (አዕመቅ "wall/hedge") is the Didascalia's expansion —
   H's apparatus cites "Isa 5, 8f."
2. The second citation (heard in the ears of the Lord of Sabaoth) echoes Amos 3:13; neither P
   nor H gives a reference at this point in the apparatus we have OCR'd. Flagged.

## 7. The landmark; Moses' charge; the fate of the unhearing and the hearers

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored):**

> ወ⌈ውስተ⌉ ⌈ድኡትኒ⌉ ⌈ደብል⌉ ⌈ርጉመ⌉ ⌈ለደ⌉ ⋅⋅⋅⋅ ⌈ዘይደበርቅ⌉ ⌈ርጉብ⌉ ⌈ቢጥ⌉ ወ⌈ደዩብል⌉ ⌈ሕዝብ⌉ ⌈ሎቱ⌉።
> ⌈ወከመዚአሆ⌉ ⌈ይቤ⌉ ⌈ሙሴ⌉ ⌈ለምእስቲ⌉ ⌈ኢትፈትው⌉ ⌈ሕደገ⌉ ⌈ትሕቲ⌉ ⌈እግዚከ⌉ ወ⌈ኢትዐቅብ⌉ ⌈ሕገ⌉ ⌈ሠርዑ⌉
> ⌈አባቲከ⌉። ⌈እም⌉ ⌈ዝንቱ⌉ ⌈ይምጽኡ⌉ ⌈ፍርሀት⌉ ወ⌈ሞት⌉ ወ⌈ሀንደሰ⌉ ወ⌈ደሪሠክቦዑ⌉ ⌈ፍርያ⌉ ⌈ይእዘብ⌉ ⌈እም⌉
> ⌈እግዚአ⌉ ብሔር ⌈ላዕለ⌉ ⌈እለ⌉ ⌈የግብር⌉ ⌈እሉ⌉። ⌈አስመ⌉ ⌈እለ⌉ ⌈የሰምዑ⌉ ⌈ገርሃተ⌉ ⌈እግዚአ⌉ ብሔር ወ⌈የንቀቡ⌉
> ⌈ሕጎ⌉ ⌈ዘበርሃነ⌉ ⌈ብእክሙ⌉ ⌈እሉ⌉ ⌈የረክቡ⌉ ⌈ሣህሎ⌉ ወ⌈ምሕረት⌉።

**Translation:**

> And in Deuteronomy: Cursed is he that removeth his neighbour's landmark; and all the people
> shall say, So be it. And for this cause Moses saith: Thou shalt not desire the boundary of thy
> neighbour, nor remove the law which thy fathers ordained. From these things come fear and
> death and confusion, and judgment overtaketh them from the Lord, upon those that do these
> things. But those who hear the word of the Lord and keep his enlightening law — these shall
> find loving-kindness and mercy.

**Notes:**
1. Dt 27:17 ("Cursed be he that removeth his neighbour's landmark. And all the people shall say,
   Amen"); P renders "So be it", H "So be it" — the Ge'ez ደዩብል/አሜን formula. H's apparatus
   cites "Dt 27,17."
2. "nor remove the law which thy fathers ordained" — an idiosyncratic conflation of Dt 19:14 /
   Pr 22:28 ("remove not the ancient landmark") with the fathers' tradition; both P and H read
   this way, so it belongs to the Ethiopic, not to Platt's fancy.
3. "fear and death and confusion" — the triad recurs in the Didascalia's warnings (cf. the
   Syriac's "confusion" terminology). H: "fear and death and confusion"; P likewise.

## 8. The golden rule and its expansions; blessing the curser

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored):**

> ⌈እስመ⌉ ⌈ይቤ⌉ ⌈ዘይእከሃ⌉ ∈⌈አንተ⌉ ⌈አ⌉ት⌈ግበር⌉ ∈⌈ብእሲትከ⌉። ወ≠⊨መ⌉ ⊢≵⍈ቅድ⌉ ∈⌈ርእስከ⌉ ⌈መርገ⌉
> ⌈ወ⌈ጽጫ∈ተት⌉ ወ⌈ዝ≥⌠≰ተት⌉ ወ⌈ከ⌈ጋ∣∆⌉ ⊨∘ ⌈አ⌉ት⌈ግ≠∭⌉ ∈⌈ር⊥∵⊨⌉⍢ ⋅⋅⋅ ⋅⋅⋅ ⋅⋅⋅ ⌈ወ≠⊨መ⌉ ⊢≵⍈ቅድ⌉
> ⌈የ⌈ስተ≅∭≣⌉ ∈⌈ብ⊥∲≵⊨⌉ ⌈≣⋕⋵⌉ ⋅⋅⋅⋅ ⋈⌈ኢ⌉ ⌈መ⊕⌈ገ⌉⍢ ⋈⌈በ⊨መ⌉ ⊢≵⍈≅ድ⌉ ⌈⌈≥∰⌉ ⌈≣⋕⋵⌉። ⋈∝⊕≰⌉
> ⌈⋘⋭⊨∵ው⊨⌉ ⌈∰≥⊥⌉ ⌈≣∭⊮⌉ ⌈እ∵∘⌉ ⌈⌽∑⍈⍈⌉ ⌈ው∵≰⌉ ⌈∘⌽∕⍈⌉ ⌈≲⌉ ⌈⊠⍖⌉ ⌈ላ⌉ ⌈⋘⊭≰⊕⌉ ⌈≡∫⊭≰⌉
> ⌈ይ⊩⊑⌉ ⌈እ∈⌉ ⌈ይ≣∭⊭ወ⊨⌉ ወ⌈ለዓ≰⌉ ⌈ይ⊩⊑⌉ ⌈እ∈⌉ ⌈ይ⊨∵ድወ⊨⌉።

**Translation:**

> For it is said: What is hateful to thyself, do not to thy neighbour. And as thou wouldest not
> that another should look upon thy wife, to turn her aside unto evil, so do not thou look upon
> the wife of another with evil thoughts. And as it pleases thee not that another take thy
> garment, so take not thou the garment of another. And as thou desirest not for thyself curse,
> and reviling, and blows, and rage, so do not thou thus unto thy neighbour. And if any man
> curse thee, bless thou him; for it is written in the book of Numbers: Blessed shall they be
> that bless thee, and cursed shall they be that curse thee.

**Notes:**
1. Tob 4:15 ("That which thou hatest, do to no man") — the negative golden rule; both P and H
   give it without naming Tobit.
2. The garment clause is an expansion of the rule into concrete cases, as in AC i.2's catalogue;
   "wife, garment, curses" parallel the covetousness list of § 4 above.
3. Num 24:9 ("Blessed is he that blesseth thee, and cursed is he that curseth thee") — spoken of
   Israel; the Didascalia transfers it to the Christian's response to revilement. The "book of
   Numbers" formula (⌈መጽ∕⍈⌉ ⌈≲⌉ ⌈⊠⍖⌉) is restored from P "the Book of Numbers" (H omits the
   book-name here).

## 9. From the Gospel: love the enemies; leave vengeance to the Lord

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored):**

> ወ⌈ከመዚአሆ⌉ ⌈ጽሑፍ⌉ ⌈ው∵ተ⌉ ⌈ወ⊕⌌∍⌉ ⌈ዘ⌈ይቤ⌉ ⌈እ⌈ግዚእ⌉ነ⌉ ⌈ይ≣∭⊭ው⌉ ⌈እ∈⌉ ⌈ይ⊨∵ድወ⊭∙⌉ ወ⌈ይ⌸∍ዩ⌉ ∈⌈እ∈⌉
> ⌈ያ⌠∝≅ወ⊭∙⌉። ⌈ዌ≥∩⌉ ⌈⌽⊕⋓≵⌉ ∈⌈እ∈⌉ ⌈የ⊭∕ድወ⊭∙⌉ ወ⌈ኢ≰∘∍∵⊭∙⌉ ⌈ክ∕ደ≰⌉ ∈⌈እ∈⌉ ⌈የ⋐∭⍈⌉ ⌈ኣ⌈ገዚ⊥⊭∙⌉
> ወ⌈ወ∅∛∎∙⌉። ⌈እ∵∘⌉ ⌈⌽∑⍈⍈⌉ ገ≥∨ ⌈ከ∎∙⋝⌉ ⋅⋅⋅⋅ ⌈ኢ≵⊕≀⌽⌉ ⌈∈∭⊥∵⊨⌉ ⌈∝∵∈⌉ ⌈⌸∋⊢⊨⌉ ⌈≠⌈⊭∕ደ≵⌉⌉
> ⋅⋅⋅⋅ ⌈ወ⊨∘ዚአ∆⌉ ⌈∈እ⌈ግዚአ⌉≥ሔ∭⌉ ⌈ይ∨ዳ⊠⊨⌉ ወ⌈ይ⌈ግ≥∭⌉ ⌈⍈∭ያ⌉ ⌈∋⋕∈⌉ ⌈⌸∋⊢⊨⌉።

**Translation:**

> And so it is written in the Gospel, our Lord said: Bless them that curse you, and pray for
> them that falsely accuse you. Do good unto them that hate you; and return not evil to them
> that wrongfully oppress you, but bear them patiently. For it is written: Thou shalt not avenge
> thyself upon thy neighbour(?) enemy for the evil that he doeth unto thee; and so the Lord shall
> help thee, and shall execute judgment upon thine enemy.

**Notes:**
1. Mt 5:44 / Lk 6:27–28 conflated; P: "Bless them that curse you, and pray for them that
   wrongfully oppress you. Do good unto them that hate you"; H agrees closely.
2. The vengeance citation conflates Pr 20:22 and Rom 12:19 / Dt 32:35 ("vengeance is mine");
   the Ge'ez ⌈∋⋕∈⌉ ⌈⌸∋⊢⊨⌉ ("upon thine enemy") makes the application personal.
3. Here the OCR of the 1834 print becomes sparse; the sentence may continue on the next
   1834 page (P: "…and will bring judgment upon thine enemies. If thou do these things, thou
   shalt abide in the right faith, and find grace and favour with Jesus Christ; for thou art
   become one of his members."). The continuation will be treated in the next file, together
   with the Proverbs vii instruction on the strange woman.
