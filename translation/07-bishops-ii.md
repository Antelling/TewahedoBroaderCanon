## 31. [ii., 5.] The bishop's judgment (Deut 1:17)

**Ge'ez (transcription, restored; Platt p. 18):**

> ⌈ወ⌉⌈ኢ⌉[⌈ር⌉⌈እ⌉⌈ይ⌉] ⌈ለ⌉ኤ⌈ጲ⌈ስ⌉ቆ⌈ጶ⌈ስ⌉ ከ⌈መ⌉ በአድልወ ⌈ባ⌉ዕል ወ⌈ዘ⌉⌈ት⌉⌈ሕ⌉⌈ደ⌉⌈ደ⌉ [⌈ለ⌉⌈ድ⌉⌈ኻ⌉]። ⌈እ⌉⌈ስ⌉⌈መ⌉
> ⌈ይ⌉⌈ቤ⌉⌈ሎ⌉ ⌈እ⌉⌈ግ⌉⌈ዚ⌉⌈አ⌉⌈ብ⌉⌈ሔ⌉⌈ር⌉ ⌈ለ⌉⌈ሙ⌉⌈ሴ⌉ አ⌈መ⌉ በ⌈አ⌉⌈ድ⌉⌈ል⌉⌈ወ⌉ ⌈ለ⌉⌈ገ⌉⌈ጸ⌉ ባ⌈ዕ⌉⌈ል⌉ ⌈ወ⌉⌈ኢ⌉⌈ት⌉⌈ኰ⌉⌈ን⌉⌈እ⌉
> ፍ⌈ት⌉⌈ሕ⌉ ⌈ነ⌉⌈ዳ⌉⌈ይ⌉ ወ⌈ፍ⌉⌈ት⌉⌈ሕ⌉ ⌈ለ⌉⌈እ⌉⌈ግ⌉⌈ዚ⌉⌈አ⌉⌈ብ⌉⌈ሔ⌉⌈ር⌉ [⌈እ⌉⌈ግ⌉⌈ዚ⌉]። [⌈ወ⌉⌈ይ⌉⌈ጋ⌉⌈ብ⌉⌈ኡ⌉] ደ⌈ፍ⌉⌈ታ⌉⌈ሕ⌉
> በ⌈ር⌉⌈ት⌉⌈ዕ⌉።

**Translation:**

> ⌈It is not right⌉ for a bishop, when he judgeth, to accept the person of the rich, and
> neglect the poor. ⌈For⌉ the Lord said unto ⌈Moses⌉: When thou judgest, accept not the
> person of the rich, ⌈and⌉ turn not aside the judgment of the poor; for ⌈judgment
> belongeth⌉ unto the Lord ⌈⌈God⌉⌉. [But he ought to judge uprightly.]

**Notes:**
1. P: "It is not good for a Bishop, when he judgeth, to accept the person of the rich, and
   to neglect the poor. For the Lord said unto Moses, When thou judgest, accept not the
   person of the rich, and turn not aside the judgment of the poor; for judgment belongeth
   unto the Lord. But it behoveth him to judge uprightly." H: "[ii., 5.] It is not right for
   the bishop to judge, accepting the person of the rich, and to neglect the poor. For God
   said unto Moses, Judge not, accepting the person of the rich, nor turn aside the judgment
   of the poor, for the judgment is the Lord's. But he ought to judge uprightly." H's
   footnote cites Deut 1:17.
2. Legible anchors: በአድልወ "accept the person" (አድለ), ባዕል "the rich", ለገጸ ባዕል, ፍትሕ
   "judgment" (twice), ወፍትሕ ለእግዚአብሔር, ለሙሴ "unto Moses" (raw "ለውጤ"), ደፍታሕ በርትዕ
   "uprightly". The print's እግዚ break after ለእግዚአብሔር suggests the Ge'ez has ⌈እግዚ⌉ "God" —
   P "the Lord", H "the Lord's", Ge'ez "the Lord God".
3. "Turn not aside": P "turn not aside", H "nor turn aside"; Ge'ez raw "ወኢ.ት σοῦ" restored
   as ወኢትኰንእ **(conj.)** (ኰነኢ, "to diminish").
4. "It is not good/right": P "It is not good", H "It is not right"; the opening verb is lost
   in the print; both render a negated ርእይ or ርግባእ **(conj.)**.

> ⌈ኵ⌉⌈ነ⌉⌈ነ⌉ ⌈ል⌉⌈ሳ⌉⌈ኑ⌉] ወ⌈ኢ⌉⌈ይ⌉⌈ደ⌉⌈ት⌉⌈ል⌉⌈ዎ⌉ [⌈ለ⌉⌈አ⌉⌈ው⌉⌈ሃ⌉⌈ት⌉] ወ⌈አ⌉⌈መ⌉ [⌈ይ⌉⌈ፈ⌉⌈ር⌉⌈ህ⌉]
> ⌈ግ⌉⌈ዚ⌉⌈አ⌉⌈ብ⌉⌈ሔ⌉⌈ር⌉።

**Translation:**

> And, moreover, let the food of the bishop be ⌈meat⌉ ⌈seasoned⌉ with ⌈wisdom⌉, ⌈that he
> may be able⌉ to teach ⌈and⌉ rebuke the multitude; ⌈and let him be far⌉ from all evil, and
> read the Scriptures ⌈at⌉ all times, and be a lover ⌈of⌉ wisdom, that he may interpret the
> Scriptures in the Holy ⌈Spirit⌉, ⌈the words of the Gospel, the Law and the prophets. For
> our Lord said: Understand ye the Scriptures, for they are witnesses concerning me. It
> becometh a bishop to search out and expound the Scriptures, and to satisfy the people with
> doctrine, and to lighten them with the law of⌉ the Lord ⌈with much admonition. As the Lord
> said: Light up upon you⌉ the light of wisdom, for it is time. ⌈Thus, then,⌉ it becometh
> the bishop ⌈to labour⌉ and toil and ⌈wrestle for righteousness' sake, and so much the more
> before the people, that they, seeing his works, may be ready to receive⌉ his ⌈teaching.
> And, moreover, let him not become surety⌉ for any man, nor ⌈make friendship with the
> great⌉, nor ⌈be⌉ of a double ⌈heart⌉ or of a double ⌈tongue, neither let him follow after
> princes, but let him fear the Lord⌉.

**Notes:**
1. P: "Let the food of the Bishop be meat seasoned with moderation, that he may be able to
   teach and rebuke the multitude, and to turn them to the Lord. And let him be far from all
   evil, and read the Scriptures at all times, and be a lover of wisdom, that he may be able
   to interpret the Scriptures, through the Holy Spirit, both the word of the Gospel, the
   Law, and the Prophets. For our Lord said, Understand ye the voice of the Scriptures, for
   they are witnesses concerning me. It becomes a Bishop to think upon searching into and
   expounding the Scriptures, in order that he may satisfy the people with doctrine, and
   water them from the light of the Law of the Lord, with much admonition. As the Lord said,
   Light up upon you the light of wisdom, for it is time. Thus then it behoveth the Bishop to
   labour and toil and wrestle for righteousness' sake; and so much the more in the presence
   of the people, that they, seeing his works, may be ready to receive his teaching. And,
   moreover, let him not become surety for any man, nor make friendship with the great, nor
   let him be of a double heart or of a double tongue; neither let him follow after princes,
   but let him fear the Lord: neither let him hear false accusation against his neighbour."
2. H (pp. 15–16): "The food of the bishop ought to be meat seasoned with moderation, that he
   may be able to teach and admonish the people, and that he may turn them to God. And let
   him keep far from all evil, and read the Scriptures at all times and be a lover of wisdom,
   that he may be able to interpret the Scriptures in the Holy Spirit, (even) the words of
   the Gospel, the Law and the prophets. The Lord saith, 'Understand ye the words of the
   Scriptures, for they bear witness of me.' It becometh a bishop to search out and expound
   the Scriptures in order, and to satisfy the people with spiritual teaching, and fill them
   with the light of the law of God with much admonition. God saith, 'Make the light of
   wisdom to shine upon you, for it is time.' [ii., 6.] Thus, then, it is right for the
   bishop to suffer and toil and wrestle for righteousness' sake, but above all before the
   people, that they, when they see his works, may receive his admonition. And, moreover, let
   him become surety for no man, nor associate with the great, nor be of a double tongue, nor
   follow after princes, but let him fear God; and let him not hearken to a false accusation
   against his neighbour."
3. Food: Ge'ez anchors ጥዑመ በዓቅም "seasoned with wisdom" — P "with moderation", H "with
   moderation"; በዓቅም can mean either "wisdom" or "moderation". P "rebuke the multitude", H
   "admonish the people": Ge'ez ለአሕዛብ "the multitude/nations" legible; ወይገሰጾ "rebuke".
   P adds "and to turn them to the Lord"; H "and that he may turn them to God" — falls in
   unrecoverable print, restored from both **(conj.)**.
4. "Voice" or "words" of the Scriptures (Jn 5:39): P "voice", H "words"; the noun is lost
   (raw "en: AIHA: AND."); the legible ⌈እ⌉⌈ሙ⌉⌈ን⌉⌈ቱ⌉ ⌈ሰ⌉⌈ም⌉⌈ዑ⌉ = እሙንቱ ሰምዑ "witnesses — hear ye"
   and በ⌈ኣ⌉⌈ን⌉⌈ቲ⌉⌈አ⌉⌈ኣ⌉⌈ሮ⌉ = በኣንቲአ [እግዚአየ] "concerning me" (H's footnote cites Jn 5:39).
5. "Satisfy the people with doctrine / spiritual teaching": the legible ⌈ት⌉⌈ም⌉⌈ህ⌉⌈ር⌉⌈ተ⌉ =
   ትምህርተ "teaching/doctrine"; whether the Ge'ez had መንፈሳዊ "spiritual" (H; his footnote "P
   omits: spiritual") is unrecoverable. "Water them / fill them": P "water them", H "fill
   them"; the verb is lost. ሕ⌈ገ⌉ ⌈ብ⌉⌈ር⌉ሃ = ሕገ ብርሃኑ "the law of his light"? P "the light of
   the Law of the Lord", H "the light of the law of God" — Ge'ez word order suggests "the
   law, its light", rendered after both.
6. "Light up upon you the light of wisdom, for it is time" — excellent anchors: ⌈አ⌉⌈ም⌉⌈ር⌉⌈ሆ⌉
   ገላ⌈ዕ⌈ሌ⌈ክ⌈ሙ⌉ ⌈ብ⌉⌈ር⌉⌈ሃ⌉⌈ነ⌉ ጥ⌈በ⌉⌈ብ⌉ ⌈እ⌉⌈ስ⌉⌈መ⌉ ⌈ጊ⌉⌈ዜ⌉⌈ሆ⌉ = አምርሆ ላዕሌክሙ ብርሃነ ጥበብ እስመ ጊዜሆ
   "light up upon you the light of wisdom, for its time". P: "Light up upon you the light of
   wisdom, for it is time"; H: "Make the light of wisdom to shine upon you, for it is time."
7. Toil: P "labour and toil and wrestle", H "suffer and toil and wrestle"; Ge'ez anchors
   ⌈ይ⌉⌈ደ⌉⌈ክ⌉⌈ም⌉ (ደከም "labour/suffer"), ወ⌈ይ⌉⌈ጻ⌉⌈ም⌉ (ጻም "toil"), ⌈ወ⌉⌈ይ⌉⌈ፈ⌉⌈ድ⌉⌈ፈ⌉⌈ድ⌉ (ፈድፈድ
   "wrestle") — all three verbs legible in the raw ("ደሕምም ወደጻሙ… ወፈድፈ"). H's footnote cites
   Lev 18:4 here ("my judgments… to walk therein"); the citation is partly garbled in our
   OCR ("Ln off.") and is recorded as far as recovered. "In the presence of the people":
   በ⌈ቅ⌉⌈ድ⌉⌈መ⌉ ሕ⌈ዝ⌉⌈ብ⌉ legible.
8. Surety clause: Ge'ez anchor ⌈ዘ⌉⌈ይ⌉⌈ት⌉⌈ሕ⌉⌈በ⌉ = ዘይትሕበ "let him not be surety" (አሕዛ).
   **Double heart and tongue:** P "of a double heart or of a double tongue"; H "of a double
   tongue" only. The raw shows two paired ኵነነ items ("ወአ.ደኩቡ HNAA : AP: OAHNAA: ATE"),
   supporting P against H's shorter text; restored as ⌈ኵነኔ ልቡ ወኢይኩን ኵነነ ልሳኑ⌉ **(conj.)**.

## 37. [ii., 6.] The close of the chapter: Solomon; the perverse generation

**Ge'ez (transcription, restored; Platt p. 22):**

> ወ⌈ሶ⌉⌈ሎ⌉⌈ሞ⌉⌈ን⌉ ⌈ይ⌉⌈ቤ⌉ ⌈ሰ⌉⌈ማ⌉⌈ዕ⌉ ገወ⌈ል⌈ድ⌉ [⌈ት⌉⌈ም⌉⌈ህ⌉⌈ር⌉⌈ተ⌉] ⌈አ⌉⌈ቡ⌉ከ ወ⌈ኢ⌉⌈ት⌉⌈ኰ⌉⌈ን⌉⌈እ⌉ [⌈ት⌉⌈እ⌉⌈ዛ⌉⌈ዘ⌉]

> And Solomon saith: Hear, my son, [the instruction of] thy father, and despise not [the
> commandment of] thy mother. And they have not heard [unto] this day, but have forsaken the

**Notes:**
1. P: "And Solomon also saith, Hear, my son, the instruction of thy father; and despise not
   the commandment of thy mother. Yet they have not heard, even unto this day; but have left
   the Lord, the only true God, and He hath called them a perverse and adulterous generation;
   of whom we have spoken before."
2. H (pp. 18–19): "And Solomon also saith, 'Hear, my son, the instruction of thy father and
   disregard not the commandment of thy mother'; yet they have not heard, even unto this
   day, but have forsaken the Lord the only true God, and He hath called them a generation of
   transgressors and adulterers, concerning whom we have spoken before." H's footnote n. 53
   cites Prov 1:8.
3. Ge'ez anchors: ወ⌈ሶ⌉⌈ሎ⌉⌈ሞ⌉⌈ን⌉ ⌈ይ⌉⌈ቤ⌉ = ወሶሎሞን ይቤ "And Solomon saith" (raw "ወዘሎሔሞኝዛ ደቤ");
   ገወ⌈ል⌈ድ⌉ = ሰማዕ ወልድ "Hear, my son" (raw "1990: ወልድ"); ወ⌈ኢ⌉⌈ት⌉⌈ኰ⌉⌈ን⌉⌈እ⌉ = ወኢትኰንኢ "and despise
   not"; ⌈እ⌉⌈ሜ⌉⌈ከ⌉ = እሜከ "of thy mother" (raw "ትእዛ ያ እም"); ወ⌈ኢ⌉⌈ሰ⌉⌈ሙ⌉⌈ዑ⌉ ዛ⌈ቲ⌉ ⌈ዕ⌉⌈ለ⌉⌈ት⌉ =
   ወኢሰሙዑ እስከ ዛቲ ዕለት "and they have not heard unto this day" (legible); ወ⌈ረ⌉⌈ደ⌉⌈ጉ⌉ = ወረደጉ
   "and have forsaken"; ዘ⌈በ⌉⌈አ⌉⌈ማ⌉⌈ን⌉ = ዘበአማን "who is in faith/truth" — P/H "the only true
   God"; the Ge'ez reads "the Lord God, who is in faith [i.e. truly]", rendered accordingly.
4. **Prov 1:8**: "instruction of thy father… commandment of thy mother" — H cites Prov 1:8;
   P's text likewise has father and mother. Ge'ez ⌈አ⌉⌈ቡ⌉ከ = አቡከ "thy father" is legible; the
   noun "instruction" is lost (raw "ተገሣጸ").
5. **Generation:** H "a generation of transgressors and adulterers" vs P "a perverse and
   adulterous generation" (a Matthean allusion, Mt 12:39/16:4 "adulterous generation"). Ge'ez
   ዐ⌈ዋ⌉⌈ል⌉⌈ያ⌉⌈ን⌉ = ዐዋልያን "the perverse" and ወ⌈ዘ⌉⌈ማ⌉⌈ው⌉⌈ያ⌉⌈ን⌉ = ወዘማውያን "and of the adulterous"
   (raw "ዐሓላውያንኝ ወዘማውያኝ") — two nouns; rendered after the Ge'ez structure, closer to H.
6. This closes Platt's Chapter III (= Harden's CHAPTER IV). The next block — Harden's

## On conventions in this file

Ge'ez is transcribed from Platt's 1834 print via OCR, then restored where the print's typesetting
collapsed. Bracket conventions: **⌈…⌉** marks text restored from OCR noise (at word level);
**[…?]** marks a word or clause lost in the print and supplied from P/H with the gap marked;
**(conj.)** marks conjectural restorations where neither English witness settles the reading.
Platt's English pagination runs offset from his Ge'ez pages — each Ge'ez page carries the text
translated on the following English page, continuously — so Platt page references in the section
headers give the Ge'ez page printed. Where P and H differ and the Ge'ez is recoverable, the
translation follows the Ge'ez; where it is not, P/H are followed and the divergence recorded in
the notes.
