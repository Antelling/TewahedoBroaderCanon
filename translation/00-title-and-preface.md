# The Didesqelya — Title and Apostolic Preface

**Source:** Ge'ez text as printed in Platt 1834 (OCR of archive.org scan `TheEthiopicDidascalia`),
checked against Platt's English and, where marked, Harden 1920 (his English translation of the
same Ethiopic; his Ge'ez pages are at archive.org `cu31924083336` spreads listed per note).
Ge'ez words restored from OCR noise are marked ⌈thus⌉; entirely uncertain words appear as
[…?] with a note.

---

## Title

**Ge'ez (transcription):**

> ድስቅልያ ዘአበው።
> ቅጁሳ ⌈ካባን⌉ ሐዋርያት በስመ እግዚአብሔር ለእግዚአብሔር ወለወልዱ ወጥዋሕድነ ⌈ቅዱስ⌉ እምነቱ ክርስቶስ ወጻራቅሊጦስ።

**Translation:**

> **The Didascalia of our Fathers.**
> *The gathering of the Apostles — in the name of God the Father, and of his only-begotten Son
> Jesus Christ, and of the Paraclete, the Holy Spirit.*

**Notes:**
1. ድስቅልያ (*dəsqəlya*) is the Ge'ez borrowing of Greek διδασκαλία. The full form ዲደስቅልያ
   *didesqəlya* appears in running heads elsewhere in the tradition; Ethiopic scribes freely
   alternate the lengths of internal vowels in Greek loanwords.
2. ⌈ካባን⌉ restores the OCR fragment `ቅጅ.ሳካባኝ` as ቅጁሳ ካባን (*qəmsa kaban*, "gathering/assembly").
   The reading is supported by the sense and by the parallel opening of the Arabic recension,
   but the exact Ge'ez wording here remains to be checked against the 1920 print.
3. ጥዋሕድነ "our only(-begotten)" — the OCR's `ወጥዋሕድ : ወ aድ ፡ እያጡ` compresses what Platt renders
   "and his only-begotten Son"; the third person possessive is supplied from his rendering.

## The Apostolic Preface

*(Harden, Additional Note A: this preface is not in the Apostolic Constitutions; it stands in
all Ethiopic manuscripts and in both recensions of the Arabic Didascalia, and in Mrs. Gibson's
Mesopotamian codex of the Syriac.)*

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored):**

> እነሆ እኛ ⌈ዾህድነ⌉ ሐዋርያት ⌈መልእክተን⌉ ለወልደ ለዋሕድ አምለክት ተካብተነ ውስተ ⌈አፍሩባሌም⌉ ሀገረ ለንጉሥ […?]
> ወምስሌነ ጳውሎስ ወንጊዜ ወንጽሕ ሐዋርያ ለኩዑ ኣሕዛብ ወያዕቆብ እምቢርሃ ለእግዚእነ ሊቀ ካህን ለዛቲ ሀገር
> እማፍሩባሌም ⌈ወሊቀ⌉ ለዛቲ ቤተ ክርስቲያን ዘበ⌈ከማ⌉ያት። ጸምኝ ከመ ንሥጡ ዘንብእ ርግበነ ኣምመኒክሙ
> ዘጽውዐነ ኢሮ ክርስቶስ ውስተ ከብሓቲሆ ከመ ንግብር ደረከት ወመንበራት ውስተ ቤተ ክርስቲያን በከመ
> ምስለ ዘሀለው ውስተ ሰማይ። ረዳአ ሊቀ ካህን ከመ ርዒተ አባግናዕ ወካህን ከመ ⌈መካሕ⌉ ወዲያቆን
> ⌈ከመ⌉ መሕደገ አርአያ ⌈ወንግርቀ⌉ ላእክ ወን⌈ግርቀ⌉ ሙዛን ወመዘምራን በጥድቅና ጺሎጣኖስ ወባዕለ
> ሕዝብ ከመ ⌈ዝሰምዖ⌉ ለቃል ወንጌል ወይሰባሕ ለምግባረ ትንሣኤ።

**Translation:**

> Behold, we the twelve Apostles, messengers of the only-begotten Son of God, gathered together
> in Jerusalem, the city of the Great King — and with us Paul, herald of glad tidings to all the
> Gentiles, and James, the brother of our Lord, chief priest of this city of Jerusalem and head
> of this church which is [the pattern] of that which is in heaven — have decreed that we
> establish the true faith, whereby our Lord Jesus Christ called us into his glory: that we
> appoint orders and seats in the Church of Christ after the pattern of that which is in heaven.
> The bishop is in the likeness of the shepherd of the sheep; the priest, in the likeness of one
> who rebukes; the deacon, in the likeness of one who serves; likewise the subdeacon; the reader,
> and the skilled singers, and all the rest of the people, in the likeness of those who hear the
> voice of the Gospel and give heed to the word of admonition.

**Notes:**
1. ሊቀ ካህን *liqa kahən*, literally "chief of priests" — the regular Ge'ez rendering of
   ἐπίσκοπος in this literature; Platt gives "Bishop", which is the function, not the etymology.
   I keep "chief priest" here for the title, "bishop" where the office is clearly meant.
2. "orders and seats" (ደረከት ወመንበራት) — *darəkät* "orders/ranks", *mänbärät* "seats"; the phrase
   answers Platt's "degrees and offices". The typology that follows (bishop = shepherd,
   deacon = minister) matches the ordering theology of AC I praef.–I.1 and, behind it, the
   column-chart of the Syriac Didascalia.
3. "herald of glad tidings" renders ወንጊዜ ወንጽሕ (*wängələ wänəṣəḥ*, lit. "preacher of the pure/
   glad announcement") — Platt: "Apostle of all the Gentiles". The Ethiopic reads Paul's office
   as evangelist rather than apostle here.
4. The clause "head of this church which is [the pattern] of that which is in heaven"
   (ለዛቲ ቤተ ክርስቲያን ዘበከማያት) is uncertain; the OCR's ዘበከማያት could also read "which is in heaven"
   (ዘበሰማያት). Platt avoids the difficulty by omitting the phrase. Flagged for checking against
   the 1920 print.
5. መካሕ "one who rebukes" and the deacon's "one who serves" (OCR lost) restore Platt's
   "reprover" and "minister"; the underlying Greek of the parallel is ἐλέγχων and διακονῶν.
