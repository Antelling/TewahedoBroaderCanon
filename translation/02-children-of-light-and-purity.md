# Section I (continued): Children of Light and the Purity of the Body

**Continues** `01-the-great-admonition.md` (which ended at the vengeance citation, "…shall
execute judgment upon thine enemy"). This file covers the Gospel word on loving enemies, the
"children of light," and Harden's section **[i., 3]**: mutual forbearance, the husband and his
wife, and the rules on hair, ointment, apparel, and the unshorn beard.
**Sigla:** P = Platt 1834 · H = Harden 1920 · AC = Apostolic Constitutions (book i).
Ge'ez restorations from OCR noise are marked ⌈thus⌉; unrecoverable words as […?]; a reading that
is conjectural is marked **(conj.)**.

---

## 10. From the Gospel: love your enemies; children of light [AC. i.]

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored):**

> ኣስመ ኣፍቅሩ ⌈ራሕተ⌉ክሙ ወጸልዩ ለእለ ⌈ያሐስሩ⌉ክሙ ከመ ትኩኑ ውሉደ ለአቡክሙ ዘበሰማያት። ⌈ኣስመ ዘያነጻሕ⌉ ⌈ፀሓይ⌉
> ⌈በእኩያን⌉ ወጻድቃን ወየወርድ ⌈ዝናብ⌉ ላዕለ ጻድቅን ወ⌈አእምርተ⌉። ⌈ለብው⌉ ⌈ንገብር⌉ ⌈ዜንቲአ⌉ ⌈ትእዛዝተ⌉ ከመ ⌈ንኩነ⌉
> ⌈ውሉደ ብርሃን⌉።

**Translation:**

> For it is said in the Gospel: Love your enemies, and pray for them that falsely accuse you,
> that ye may be the children of your Father who is in heaven; for he maketh his sun to rise
> upon the evil and the good, and sendeth rain upon the just and the unjust. Beloved, let us
> keep these commandments, that we may be children of light.

**Notes:**
1. Mt 5:44b–45. P: "Love your enemies, and pray for them that persecute you"; H: "pray for
   them that persecute you." The Ge'ez verb restored here as ⌈ያሐስሩ⌉ (from ሐሰረ/ሐሰየ,
   "falsely accuse") fits the conflation of Lk 6:28 already noted in §9 above; P's
   "persecute" reads the Ge'ez ዘያርዱ differently. The print is too noisy to decide.
2. P: "children of your Father which is in heaven"; H: "sons of your Father." Ge'ez ውሉደ is
   literally "offspring/children" — P's rendering is the more literal.
3. "children of light": P "the children of light"; H "sons of light." Ge'ez restored
   ⌈ውሉደ ብርሃን⌉ (conj.); the formula echoes Lk 16:8 / Jn 12:36 and prepares [i., 3]'s
   "children of light and of God."
4. This whole subsection (with §§ 6–9) stands in AC i.2–3 in an abbreviated form; the
   sun-and-rain clause is absent from AC.

## 11. [i., 3.] Bear with one another; the husband and his wife [AC. i.]

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored):**

> ⌈ተሳብሩ⌉ ⌈ምስለ⌉ ⌈አሕድክሙ⌉ ወልድ ⌈ብርሃን⌉ ወ⌈ውሉድ⌉ ⌈እግዚአ⌉ብሔር። ወብእሲ ⌈ይሰብር⌉ ⌈ከመ⌉ ምስለ ⌈ብእሲቱ⌉ ወኢይኩን
> ⌈ውቀተ⌉ ወኢይኩን ⌈ከድያዊ⌉። ⌈እላ⌉ ⌈ርሕም⌉ ወ⌈ቀናእ⌉ ወይሕፊ ለ⌈ባሕቲቱ⌉ በፍቅር ወበ⌈ትሕትና⌉። ወኢየአድም ወ⌈አትፎደፍ⌉ቱ
> ⌈በካልእ አንስት⌉ ከመ ⌈ኢትርድእ⌉ሆ ውስተ ⌈ድኩመት⌉።

**Translation:**

> Bear with one another, O children of light and children of God. And let a man too bear with
> his wife, and be not haughty, neither false; but compassionate and upright, and let him
> cherish her alone in love and in humility. And let him not desire, nor take pleasure in,
> another woman, lest she lead him astray into evil.

**Notes:**
1. P: "Bear with one another, O children of light, and children of God! And let a man, too,
   bear with his wife, and not be haughty or false; but let him be compassionate and upright,
   and cherish her alone in love and gentleness"; H: "Bear patiently with one another, O ye
   sons of God. And let the husband also bear patiently with his wife, and not be haughty or
   false; but let him be merciful and upright, and cherish her alone in love and humility."
   The Ge'ez supports both "children of light and of God" (P) — ⌈ወልድ ብርሃን ወውሉድ እግዚአብሔር⌉
   (conj.); P here is fuller than H.
2. "haughty or false": Ge'ez ⌈ውቀተ⌉ (literally "pride") and ⌈ከድያዊ⌉ (liar) restored from noise;
   both versions agree.
3. "lest she lead him astray unto evil" (H) is Platt's omission; Ge'ez ⌈ከመ ኢትርድእሆ ውስተ
   ድኩመት⌉ (conj.) supports H, "into evil."
4. In AC the parallel passage (i.3) has only the general "Bear patiently with one another";
   the husband-and-wife application is an Ethiopian/Syriac expansion, cf. Additional Note A.

## 12. [i., 3.] If thou committest adultery, thou shalt die the death [AC. i.]

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored):**

> ወእመ ተዘማ ወገብርክ ዜንቱ ⌈ትሙት⌉ ⌈ሙትን⌉ በት ፍርሀተ ⊦⌈ግዚአ⌉ብሔር ⌈ይመጽእ⌉ ላዕለከ ወ⌈ትኩን⌉ ⌈ኵሉከ⌉ ≠⌈ርኩስ⌉ ≠⌈ርኩስ⌉።
> ወእመ ታስተብእስ እኩየ ውስተ ልብከ ወኢተዘምክ ምስሌሃ ⌈ይኩን⌉ ሎት ኃጢአት። ወእመ ተዳልክ ወ⌈ተጌጥክ⌉ ከመ ትረከበ ብእሲተ
> ⌈ገባርከ⌉ ፍቅረከ ⌈ኃጥአክ⌉ ወኮንከ ⌈ስንእስት⌉ እሙንታ ወገብርካ ለ⌈ደባ⌉ ተዝማ። ወእለ ⌈ረከበ⌉ በውሕሰት ሎቱ ጽድቶ እግዚአ
> ≠⌈ርኩስ⌉፣ ≠⌈ዝሙት⌉፣ ⋯ እስከ ከመ ⌈ይኩን⌉ ⊦⌈ግዚአ⌉ብሔር ወ⌈አትችቸፈለጥ⌉ ⊠⌈ምጌ⌉ሆ⌈ሁ⌉።

**Translation:**

> But if thou committest adultery, and doest thus, thou shalt surely die the death; and the
> fear of God shall come upon thee, and thou shalt be condemned with a bitter condemnation. And
> if thou thinkest evil in thy heart, and hast not committed adultery with her, it shall be sin
> unto thee. And if thou preparest and adornest thyself, that the wife of thy neighbour may
> love thee, thou hast sinned, and art become unto her a stumbling-block, and hast made her
> commit adultery. And for this cause shall judgment find thee; for God hath said, Thou shalt
> not desire, and thou shalt not commit adultery; and thou hast grieved the Spirit of God who
> ⌈fashioned⌉ thee.

**Notes:**
1. H: "But if thou committest adultery, and doest thus, thou shalt surely die, and the wrath of
   God shall come upon thee, and thou shalt be condemned with a bitter condemnation. But if
   thou thinkest evil in thy heart, and committest not adultery with her, it shall be sin unto
   thee. And if thou hast prepared and adorned thyself, that the wife of thy neighbour may love
   thee, thou hast erred and hast become a stumbling-block unto her, and made her to commit
   adultery. And for this cause condemnation shall come upon thee, for God saith, Thou shalt
   not desire, and Thou shalt not commit adultery." P is close throughout ("die the death",
   "judgment of God shall come upon thee").
2. The double commandment ("Thou shalt not desire… not commit adultery") = Ex 20:17 + 20:13
   (Harden's references). "Thou hast grieved the Spirit of God" — restored from
   ⌈ወአትችቸፈለጥ ኣምጌሁሆሁ⌉, echoes Eph 4:30 ("grieve not the holy Spirit of God"); H and P both omit
   this clause, so the restoration is conjectural (conj.).
3. "stumbling-block" (⌈ስንእስት⌉): the same word recurs in § 13 below (the bath) and in § 1
   above (those who heed not the law); cf. Mt 18:7 / Rom 14:13.

## 13. [i., 3.] Purity of the body: hair, ointment, and apparel [AC. i.]

**Ge'ez (transcription, partly restored):**

> ⌈ወትብ⌉እ በ⌈ንጽሕ⌉ ⊠⌈ትሐ⌉⌈መ⌉ን እስከ ከመ ⌈ትርከብ⌉ ዕረፍተ ወሕይወተ ⋯ በ⌈ኘበ⌉ ⊦⌈ግዚአ⌉ብሔር። እስመ ⌈ይቤ⌉ ⊠⌈ትረከብ⌉ ⌈ፀጉረ⌉
> ⌈ርእሰ⌉ከ ረዚእ ወ⌈ቁርጸ⌉ሆ ወ⌈ንጽሕ⌉ ⌈ርእስከ⌉ ከመ ⌈ትእተዎ⌉ ለ⊦⌈ግዚአ⌉ብሔር ወ⌈አትፍለ⌉ጥ እ⌈ምንቱ⌉። ወአንተ አ⌈ትሽ⌉⌈ተፍ⌉ በ⌈ማዕበል⌉
> ⌈ሐልወ⌉ ወኢትልበስ አ⌈ምብ⌉ረ ⊫⌈ርጐ⌉ ለአ⌈ንስተ⌉ እሎቱ ትድለዮሙ። ወ⌈አትኩን⌉ ⌈ራዕይ⌉ ወ⌈አትኩን⌉ ⌈ዘየገብርከ⌉ ስንእስት ወ⌈እንተ⌉
> ⌈ትብእ⌉ ⌈ተጥብ⌉። ጠ⌈ፈሎሃ⌉ ወ⌈አትወለድ⌉ሆ እስመ ⌈ዝንቱ⌉ ⊵⌈ሉ⌉ ተ⊠⌈ምር⌉ተ ∭⊪⌈ስ⌉ ወዝ⌈ሙት⌉ ⋍⌈ሓቱ⌉። እስመ ⌈ጽሑፍ⌉ ውስተ ⌈ደገ⌉ም
> ⌈ሕግ⌉ ⋯ ወ⊠⌈ትል⌉በስ ∍⌈ብሰ⌉ ⋯ ዘ⌈ደማ⌉ዝ⌈መ⌉ ሥ⌈ር⌉ ወ⌈ስሂ⌉። ወ≣⌈ሕቱ⌉ ⌈ርእይት⌉ወ ⌈አይብሐ⌉ጥ ∰⋕⌈ሎ⌉ት ⊥⌈ግዚአ⌉ብ∔⌈ር⌉
> ⋍⌈ስተ⌉ ⌈ቅርጽ⌉ ⊫⌈ልእ⌉ ር።

**Translation:**

> But enter thou in purity ⌈to please⌉, that thou mayest find rest and life eternal in the
> presence of God the Lord. For it is said: Let not the hair of thy head grow long, but cut it,
> and purify thy head, that thou mayest serve God and not be separated from him. And do not
> thou anoint thyself with perfumed ointment, nor clothe thyself in gorgeous apparel, wherewith
> thou mayest lead women astray; and be not a praiser of thyself, nor become an occasion of
> stumbling, nor, when thou goest, go in adorned(?) state. Plait it not, neither curl it, for
> all this is a sign of impurity and of adultery. For it is written in Deuteronomy in the law:
> ⌈thou shalt not put on a garment⌉ … woven ⌈of wool and flax⌉ together. But we ought not to
> shave the hair of our heads before God(?) ⌈nor to change the nature of man into another
> fashion⌉.

**Notes:**
1. H: "But thou oughtest in purity to please God, that thou mayest find rest and life eternal
   before God. For it is said, Let not the hair of thy head grow long, but cut (it) and purify
   thyself, that thou mayest serve God and not be separated from Him. But do not thou anoint
   thyself with perfumed ointment, nor clothe thyself with gorgeous apparel to lead women
   astray, nor vaunt thyself, nor become an occasion of sin, but seek purity and well-doing.
   And thou, O man of God, let not the hair of thy head grow long; plait (it) not, nor curl
   (it), for all this is a sign of impurity and adultery. For it is written in Deuteronomy, And
   thou shalt not put on a garment which is woven with wool and flax: thou shalt make for thyself
   a twisted fringe for thy garment. But we ought not to shave our beards, nor to change the
   nature of man into another fashion."
2. The doubleti in H ("let not the hair grow long" twice, cf. his note "This is a mistake")
   reproduces a repetition in the Ge'ez tradition; the Platt Ge'ez here is too noisy to decide
   whether the repetition stood in P's copy.
3. Dt 22:11 (wool and flax) + 22:12 (twisted fringe); the Ge'ez for the "twisted fringe"
   clause is not recovered from the print […?]. AC i.2 cites the same two verses (where the
   Greek Didascalia said: "neither curl thy hair nor shave…").
4. The closing clause is restored from the noise ⌈AMO ደቤ ATS LOCT CANN AA συν ቅርጽ ካልእ ር⌉ =
   "…ኢይብሐጥ ሰዕሎት እግዚአብሔር ውስተ ቅርጽ ካልእ ወአትወልጥ" (conj.): "not to shave … before God … into
   another fashion"; P: "shave"; H: "shave our beards."
5. P's section-head for what follows, "I. That it is the duty of the Rich to search into the
   profit of the Scriptures at all times (in the strength of) the Holy Spirit," is covered in
   the next file (`03-love-the-lord-and-the-rich.md`).
