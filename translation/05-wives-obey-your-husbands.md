## 23. [i., 8.] The head-chain; "be in subjection with humility"

**Ge'ez (transcription, heavily restored; the 1834 page is badly broken here):**

> ⌈ርእ⌉⌈ሰ⌉ ⌈ብ⌉⌈እ⌉⌈ሲ⌉ ⋯ ወ⌈ር⌉⌈እ⌉⌈ሰ⌉ ⌈አ⌉⌈ን⌉⌈ስ⌉⌈ቲ⌉ ወ⌈ር⌉⌈እ⌉⌈ሰ⌉ ዘ⌈በ⌉ ካ⌈ብ⌉⌈ር⌉ት ክ⌈ር⌉⌈ስ⌉⌈ቶ⌉⌈ስ⌉ ወ⌈ር⌉⌈እ⌉⌈ሰ⌉ ክ⌈ር⌉⌈ስ⌉⌈ቶ⌉⌈ስ⌉
> ⌈እ⌉⌈ግ⌉⌈ዚ⌉⌈አ⌉⌈ብ⌉⌈ሔ⌉⌈ር⌉ አ⌈ብ⌉ […?] ዘ⌈ለ⌉⌈ዓ⌉⌈ለ⌉ ⌈ኵ⌉⌈ሉ⌉ ዘ⌈ኮ⌉⌈ነ⌉ ወ⌈ዘ⌉⌈ይ⌉⌈ኵ⌉⌈ን⌉ ወ⌈ዘ⌉⌈ይ⌉⌈ኵ⌉⌈ን⌉ ⌈ገ⌉⌈ብ⌉⌈ረ⌉ ⌈ኵ⌉⌈ሉ⌉

**Translation:**

> ⌈The head of a wife is her husband, and the head of a man that walketh in the right way is
> Christ, and the head of Christ is God⌉ the Father, […? who is above all, who was and is and is
> to come, Creator of all things, together with his Son Jesus Christ our Lord, unto whom be
> majesty and praise and glory]. Ye women therefore, be obedient to your husbands and be subject
> to them in humility and in the fear of God, as we said before when we taught you, that ye may
> find favour with them.

**Notes:**
1. P: "Let the Woman be subject to her Husband; for the head of the woman is the man; and the
   head of the man who walketh in the right way, is Christ; and the head of Christ is God the
   Father, who is above all, who was and is to come, the Creator of all things, together with
   his son Jesus Christ our Lord, unto whom be majesty and praise and glory. Ye women then be
   obedient to your husbands, and be subject in humility and in the fear of the Lord. And please
   your husbands with good service, that ye may find favour with them, as we have said before."
   H: "Let the wife obey and be subject to her husband, for 'the head of a wife is her husband',
   and the head of the man that walketh in the right way (is) Christ, and the head of Christ
   (is) God: even above all, who was and is and is to come, the Creator of all things together
   with his Son Jesus Christ our Lord, to whom be majesty and praise and glory. Be in subjection
   with humility and honour and the fear of God; and delight your husbands with service, that ye
   may find favour with them, as our Lord said unto you." (H's closing "as our Lord said unto
   you" differs from P's "as we have said before"; the Ge'ez ⌈አ⌉⌈ም⌉⌈ር⌉⌈ክ⌉⌈ና⌉ "we said" supports P,
   but the page is too broken to decide.)
2. 1 Cor 11:3 underlies the chain. The raw print reads only
   "nn በሞኝ … WANT ጽንዕት … AVON ዘበ ካብርት … PAT ኣምዕ እጭ … HHS Ud", which I restore as
   ርእሰ/ወርእሰ/አንስቲ/ክርስቶስ/እግዚአብሔር (conj.). The gloss ዘበ ካብርት ("in the superior?") corresponds
   to "walketh in the right way" in both Englishes; possibly ዘይሄውር በፍኖተ ጽድቅ (conj.).
3. H's footnote: "¹ this witness" attaches to the head-chain (Harden, p. 9 n. 1). The order in
   the Ge'ez print (man → woman) cannot be verified; the Englishes read woman → man.

**Translation:**

> But it is right for faithful women that they veil their heads in purity, and not enhance the
> beauty of their faces with ⌈paints⌉ and ⌈colouring⌉ and ornaments, which have no profit in the
> body which God created, ⌈adding thus⌉. ⌈But⌉ when they walk in the way, let them ⌈turn down⌉
> their faces ⌈and⌉ ⌈be⌉ ⌈veiled⌉. Rise up, ⌈O faithful women⌉, and speak no word of ⌈contention⌉
> with any one, ⌈and above all⌉ not with your own husbands. Worship God with joy and gladness.
> And ye also, our kinsmen and our children and our ⌈neighbours⌉ and ⌈our beloved⌉, seek out the
> wisdom which is precious, which ⌈maketh straight⌉ our minds, ⌈and lighteneth our hearts⌉, and
> bringeth us near unto God, and giveth us ⌈entrance⌉ into the kingdom of heaven, into ⌈life⌉
> everlasting, and ⌈rest⌉.

**Notes:**
1. P: "But it is right for faithful women to veil their heads with purity, not setting off the
   beauty of their faces with paints, and adding colouring powder and ornaments, which are
   unprofitable, to the form which God hath created: let them not do thus. But when they walk in
   the way, let them turn their faces downward, being veiled. Take heed to yourselves, O faithful
   women, and talk not contentiously with any one, and above all not with your own husbands. O
   ye good and humble and virtuous women, worship the Lord with joy and rejoicing. And you also,
   our brethren, and our children, and kinsmen, and well-beloved, search into that glorious
   wisdom which directeth our minds aright, and enlighteneth our hearts, and bringeth us near
   unto the Lord, and giveth us entrance into the kingdom of heaven, into everlasting rest."
2. H (pp. 12–13): "But it is right for faithful women to veil themselves in purity, not seeking
   to enhance the beauty of their faces with dyes and painting and adornment, which have no
   profit, on the form which God created. Let them not do thus, but when they walk in the way
   let them incline their heads, being veiled. [i., 10.] And take heed to yourselves, ye faithful
   women, and be no (word of) hate to anyone, but above all (not) to your husbands. O ye good
   and gentle women, worship God with joy and gladness; and ye also, our children and our
   brethren and kinsmen and beloved, seek after the precious wisdom that adorneth our life, and
   enlighteneth our hearts, and bringeth us close to God, and winneth for us an entrance into
   the Kingdom of heaven in life eternal and rest."
3. Harden's [i., 10.] begins at "take heed to yourselves"; the veiling instruction before it has
   no marker in these pages and is here assigned to [i., 9.] provisionally. ⌈ዘ⌉⌈ተ⌉⌈ገ⌉⌈ለ⌉⌈በ⌉⌈ባ⌉
   = ዘተገለበዋ "that they veil (themselves)" — ገለበ, "to veil".
4. "dyes and painting and adornment" = ጥ⌈ም⌉⌈ዕ⌉⌈ታ⌉⌈ት⌉ ("paints", from ጸሐየ?) ወ⌈ተ⌉⌈ው⌉⌈ካ⌉⌈ር⌉⌈ጉ⌉ (from
   ካረገ, "to colour/dye" — a loan from Ar. كرك "henna"?) ወ⌈ዕ⌉⌈ለ⌉⌈ታ⌉⌈ት⌉ ("ornaments"). "which have no
   profit in the body which God created" = ⋘አ⌈ል⌉⌈ቦ⌉ ⌈ፋ⌉⌈ይ⌉⌈ዳ⌉ ው⌈ስ⌉⌈ተ⌉ ⌈አ⌉⌈ካ⌉⌈ል⌉ ዘ⌈ፈ⌉⌈ጠ⌉⌈ረ⌉ ⌈እ⌉⌈ግ⌉⌈ዚ⌉⌈አ⌉⌈ብ⌉⌈ሔ⌉⌈ር⌉ —
   ፋይዳ is a loanword (Pg. faida / Ar. فائدة "profit, advantage").
5. The section ends this file; H's CHAPTER IV (Platt Ch. III), "OF BISHOPS, PRESBYTERS, AND
   DEACONS" [i., 11.], is next in `06-bishops.md`.
