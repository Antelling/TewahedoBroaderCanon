   ወዘኢመሐሉ "and who turn not back" restored **(conj.)**. The Gehenna clause is wholly lost
   (raw "DAICH"); restored from P/H **(conj.)**. Platt's chapter opening here has no title
   line in the print (the raw shows the running head ትምህርተ ደድስቅልያ ዘአበው only).
4. H's [ii., 7.] marker stands at this sentence (his p. 19 column); P has no marker here.

> [⌈And others⌉ that [⌈have not believed⌉], [⌈when they believe and are baptized⌉] become
> ⌈blessed⌉ before God. ⌈As our Lord said in the Gospel: Blessed are ye, when they⌉

**Notes:**
1. P (pp. 22–23): "And there are others, also, who have not been believers; but afterwards,
   when they have been baptized, they become blessed before the Lord; as our Lord said in the
   Gospel, Blessed are ye when men shall persecute you, and revile you, and speak all evil
   against you falsely, for my sake: rejoice and be glad, for great is your reward in heaven.

   reward in heaven.' And if there be anyone against whom they have spoken, blessed is he.
   For it is written, saying, 'Every man who is not tried, is not elect before God.' If there
   be any man who hath transgressed the law, and men curse him, he hath brought the curse on
   his own head. In like manner we also, if we hear not the commandments of God and do not
   His will, shall bring a curse upon ourselves, and moreover shall be far from God." H's
   footnotes (our OCR, p. 19): "P adds: and be glad"; "Mt 5, 11"; "P adds: falsely"; "P

   "persecute you, and revile you" against H's "revile you and mock you"; P's "falsely" is
   H's addition per his footnote, and the Ge'ez [⌈በ⌉⌈ኣ⌉⌈ን⌉⌈ቲ⌉⌈አ⌉⌈የ⌉ ተ⌈ፈ⌉⌈ሥ⌉⌈ሑ⌉] (raw "ዩደሔስከው

   and be glad" vs H's "Rejoice" (his n.: "P adds: and be glad"); the Ge'ez tail
   [⌈ተ⌈ፈ⌉⌈ሥ⌉⌈ሑ⌉ እ⌈ስ⌉⌈መ⌉ ⌈ዋ⌉⌈ሕ⌉⌈ብ⌉⌈ክ⌉⌈ሙ⌉ ብ⌈ዙ⌉⌈ሣ⌉ ው⌈እ⌉⌈ቱ⌉ በ⌈ስ⌉⌈ማ⌉⌈ያ⌉⌈ት⌉] (raw "ተፈሥሑ ኣስመ ሀስብ ብዙሣ ውእቱ
   በስማያት") preserves "rejoice, for great is your reward in heaven"; whether the Ge'ez had
   both verbs is unrecoverable.
4. **The untried man:** P "not chosen before the Lord", H "not elect before God"; the Ge'ez

5. "The commandments of God / of the Lord": P "the commandments of the Lord", H "the

   "Driven far" (P) vs "far" (H): Ge'ez ⌈ር⌉⌈ሑ⌉⌈ቅ⌉ = ርሕቅ "far" is legible and neutral.

**Notes:**
1. P (Platt English pp. 22–23): "For those who do not what themselves teach, neither turn from
   following their own ways, such men the Bishop ought to reprove and put to shame. But he must
   teach them, in the love of Christ, how they ought to be obedient. The Bishop ought to seek
   after righteousness, and not honour transgressors, nor shew partiality in judgment, nor
   receive gifts from any one; for a gift blindeth the eyes of the wise: nor must he pervert the
   right cause. And, moreover, it is said in a certain place, Remove from yourselves all evil.
   And Solomon also saith, Go not to the place where they pitch their tents; come not thither;
   remove from them, and turn back. For they sleep not, unless they do evil; sleep is taken away
   from them, and they sleep not who devour iniquity."
2. H (pp. 18–19): "For as for such as do not what they themselves teach, and turn not from
   their own ways, [it] behoves [the bishop] to reprove them and put to shame those who do
   thus. It is right that he teach them in the love of Christ how they ought to serve.
   [ii., 9.] It is right for the bishop to seek after righteousness, and not honour
   transgressors, nor be partial in judgment, nor take a bribe from any man, for a bribe
   blindeth the eyes of the wise, nor turn aside a right cause. And moreover it is said
   somewhere, 'Put away all evil from you.' And Solomon also saith, 'Come not into the place
   where they encamp; withdraw from them, and turn away. For they sleep not, unless they do
   evil. Sleep is taken away from them, and they sleep not who feed on sin.'" H's **[ii., 9.]**
   marker stands at "seek after righteousness": the reprove/teach sentences close [ii., 8.] in
   his numbering. P has no marker here.
3. H's note at "seek after": "**P:** persecute" — H records Platt's English as "persecute" (the
   older sense "pursue"); our scan of Platt's print reads "seek after", so the divergence cannot
   be confirmed from the 1834 text. The Ge'ez is too damaged to settle: raw "ርቱዕ" (twice;
   also "ርቱህ") possibly from ረድዓ "pursue" **(conj.)**, but no more.
4. Ge'ez anchors (this page is among the most damaged in the print): እ⌈ስ⌉⌈መ⌉
   ⌈ዘ⌉⌈ለ⌉⌈ሒ⌉⌈ሊ⌉⌈ሆ⌉⌈ሙ⌉ (raw; perh. ዘይመሐርዎሙ "who teach them" **(conj.)**); ወ⌈ኢ⌉⌈ደ⌉⌈ገ⌉⌈ብ⌉⌈ረ⌉
   "and turned not back"; ወ⌈ኢ⌉⌈ደ⌉⌈ት⌉⌈ቀ⌉⌈መ⌉⌈ኮ⌉⌈ጡ⌉ (unclear); ወ⌈ይ⌉⌈ከ⌉⌈ስ⌉⌈ተ⌉⌈ን⌉⌈ቆ⌉⌈ሙ⌉ "and put them to
   shame" (raw "የከስተኝኘፍሮመሙ"); ከ⌈መ⌉⌈ዝ⌉ "thus" (raw "ደገብረ ከመዝ", perh. እለ ዘይገብርዎ ከመዝ "those who do
   thus" — H's phrasing); ለ⌈ዐ⌉⌈ላ⌉⌈ው⌉⌈ያ⌉⌈ን⌉ ≈ ለዐዋልያን "transgressors"; በ⌈ው⌉⌈ስ⌉⌈ተ⌉ ፍ⌈ት⌉⌈ሕ⌉ "in
   judgment"; እ⌈ም⌉⌈እ⌉⌈ን⌉⌈ት⌉⌈ክ⌉⌈ም⌉ "from yourselves" — the **last word of Ge'ez p. 22** (raw
   "አምኔጌኔካ"), showing that the "Put away all evil from you" quotation closed the page; ወ⌈ሰ⌉⌈ሎ⌉⌈ሞ⌉⌈ን⌉
   ⌈ይ⌉⌈ቤ⌉ "And Solomon saith" opens Ge'ez p. 23 (raw "ወሰሎሞኝሂ ደቢ"); ኢ⌈ት⌉⌈ም⌉⌈ጻ⌉⌈ኡ⌉ "come not
   near" (raw "ኢፐምጻኽ"); እ⌈ኩ⌉⌈ያ⌉⌈ን⌉ "the wicked" (raw "እኩያ"); ን⌈ዋ⌉⌈ም⌉ "sleep"; ደ⌈በ⌉⌈ል⌉⌈ዑ⌉ ≈
   ይበልዑ "who devour" (raw "ደበልዑ").
5. "Be obedient" (P) vs "serve" (H): the verb is lost; restored ይትአምር "be obedient, hear"
   **(conj.)** per P. "A gift blindeth the eyes of the wise" (P "gifts… a gift blindeth the eyes
   of the wise", H "a bribe blindeth the eyes of the wise"): raw is garbage here; the clause is
   supplied **(conj.)** from P/H. The "somewhere / in a certain place" framing of the quotation is
   likewise unrecoverable (raw garbage before "አምኔጌኔካ").
6. **Prov 4:14–16** (Ethiopic numbering; H gives no reference): P "Go not to the place where
   they pitch their tents; come not thither; remove from them, and turn back. For they sleep
   not, unless they do evil; sleep is taken away from them, and they sleep not who devour
   iniquity" vs H "Come not into the place where they encamp; withdraw from them, and turn
   away… they sleep not who feed on sin." Ge'ez: "ኢትምጻኡ እሎሙ" supports the P/H shared core;
   "tents / encamp" (raw "መጣኝ ኣኝተ") and "iniquity / sin" (raw "ግጢአተ", unclear) are
   unrecoverable; P's extra clause "come not thither" cannot be confirmed.

## On conventions in this file

Ge'ez is transcribed from Platt's 1834 print via OCR, then restored where the print's typesetting
collapsed. Bracket conventions: **⌈…⌉** marks text restored from OCR noise (at word level);
**[…?]** marks a word or clause lost in the print and supplied from P/H with the gap marked;
**(conj.)** marks conjectural restorations where neither English witness settles the reading.
Platt's English pagination runs offset from his Ge'ez pages — each Ge'ez page carries the text
translated on the following English page, continuously — so Platt page references in the section
headers give the Ge'ez page printed. Where P and H differ and the Ge'ez is recoverable, the
translation follows the Ge'ez; where it is not, P/H are followed and the divergence recorded in
the notes.
